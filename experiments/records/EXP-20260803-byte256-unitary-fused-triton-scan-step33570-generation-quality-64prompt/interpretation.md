# Epoch-10 H1 shallow-quality result

The fixed 64-prompt producer was rerun with only the checkpoint changed to
step 33570.  This interpretation uses H1 and the first four generated bytes
only.

| H1 measure | Epoch 3 | Epoch 5 | Epoch 10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| validation NLL | 1.51911 | 1.48307 | 1.39760 |
| greedy first-byte accuracy | 59.375% | 62.500% | 67.188% |
| greedy aligned accuracy, first 4 bytes | 40.625% | 41.406% | 46.875% |
| greedy mean exact-prefix length | 1.547 | 1.766 | 2.031 bytes |
| greedy maximum exact-prefix length | 7 | 8 | 9 bytes |
| top-p first-byte accuracy | 56.250% | 59.375% | 62.500% |
| top-p aligned accuracy, first 4 bytes | 32.422% | 34.375% | 41.406% |

From epoch 5 to epoch 10, greedy gains three correct first-byte predictions
out of 64 and fourteen aligned bytes out of the 256 first-four-byte positions.
The NLL reduction is also larger than the epoch-3-to-5 change.  This is a real
improvement in shallow conditional quality rather than metric noise alone.

Strong local examples at epoch 10 include:

- `animal -> s , and `, preserving the exact reference for eight bytes;
- `against t -> he`;
- `Richar -> d`;
- a prompt ending in `of` continuing with the exact local phrase ` the `.

The remaining errors are primarily contextual selection rather than inability
to form text:

- the U2 context expects `punk rock`, but begins `the state`;
- `Turkish forces` is replaced by `state of the`;
- `separate conti...` is still completed as `continued` rather than
  `contingents`;
- `advoca...` regresses from the epoch-5 exact `ted ` to the plausible but
  wrong `te `.

The defensible H1-only conclusion is that epoch 10 has materially better
immediate morphology, punctuation, function-word choice, and short-prefix
continuation than epochs 3 and 5.  It still often chooses a generic
grammatically possible continuation instead of the context-specific word.
There is no shallow evidence of a qualitative semantic phase transition, but
the H1 base predictor is continuing to improve rather than remaining at its
epoch-5 level.
