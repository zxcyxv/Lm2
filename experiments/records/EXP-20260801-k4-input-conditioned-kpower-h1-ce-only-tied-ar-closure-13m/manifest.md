## Post-run scope correction

The original monitor in this record froze the matrix generated at the initial
boundary and evaluated `K(h_0)^j h_0`. After the run, the user clarified that
“power” meant state-dependent self-composition:

`u_(j+1) = K(u_j) u_j`.

Therefore this record's training loss and checkpoints remain valid h1-CE
evidence, but its h2--h4 closure metrics and byte-follow-up verdict do not
answer the intended question. They are preserved as a block-frozen ablation.
The corrected checkpoint audit is recorded in
`EXP-20260801-k4-input-conditioned-self-composition-ar-audit-13m`.

## Question

When an exact-inverse encoder/decoder model has no independent LM-head,
generates an input-conditioned linear operator, and is trained only by
one-step tied-codebook CE, does the initial operator orbit become consistent
with the model's own greedy autoregressive orbit through horizon four?

This run tests that question directly. It does **not** compare the parallel
states with hidden states obtained by inserting held-out correct future
tokens.

## Compared sequences

At a shared literal prefix boundary define

`h_0 = E(x_<=t)[-1]` and `K_0 = K(h_0)`.

The one-shot parallel sequence freezes that initial operator for the whole
block:

`p_j = K_0^j h_0`, for `j = 1..4`.

The reference AR sequence is generated by the model itself. At each step it
decodes the current proposal, chooses the tied-codebook greedy argmax, appends
that chosen token, canonically re-encodes the complete generated prefix, and
regenerates the input-conditioned operator:

`a_j = argmax TiedCodebook(D(K(c_(j-1)) c_(j-1)))`

`c_j = E(x_<=t, a_1, ..., a_j)[-1]`, with `c_0 = h_0`.

The primary comparison is `p_j` versus `c_j`. Thus the reference is the
sequential greedy AR process, not teacher-forced future hidden states.

## Architecture and objective

- BPE-8192 WikiText-103 input
- width 896, two reversible encoder blocks
- analytic exact inverse as decoder
- no separately parameterized vocabulary projection
- output scores are `RMSNorm(decoded) @ embedding.T`; the input embedding is
  the only token codebook
- one prefix-conditioned real-normal linear operator per root; the operator
  is frozen over each one-shot four-state parallel block
- spectral alpha zero, so the monitored sequence is the literal unprojected
  linear orbit `K_0^j h_0`
- training objective: next-token CE at horizon one only
- no latent MSE, future-state regression, KL, branch, innovation, sampled
  noise, token feedback inside the trained one-step path, or h2--h4 loss

Held-out next tokens are used only as ordinary CE labels and optional NLL
diagnostics. Held-out future hidden states are not an evaluation target.

## Preflight criteria

- no independent vocabulary-sized linear head exists
- the active tied codebook retrieves its own embedding rows
- the closed-form four-step orbit matches literal repeated application of
  the initial input-conditioned operator
- the initial operator varies across roots
- changing future labels leaves roots, generated operators, and predictions
  unchanged
- CE gives finite nonzero gradients to the operator controller, spectral
  basis, reversible encoder/decoder, and tied embedding
- one-step parallel and AR logits agree numerically before AR feedback

## Success and follow-up rule

For each horizon, record parallel/AR token agreement and, between `p_j` and
`c_j`, cosine similarity, relative MSE, and the rate with cosine at least
0.99. The run counts as sustained closure improvement only if:

1. at least three of four horizons have higher final cosine than at step 0;
2. at least three of four horizons have lower final relative MSE than at
   step 0;
3. the median four-horizon cosine at steps 500, 750, and 1000 is above its
   step-0 value; and
4. the final median cosine improvement is at least 0.01.

If these criteria fail, the next experiment changes tokenization to a
byte-level codebook while preserving the architecture, objective, AR
reference, horizon four, and reporting definitions as closely as possible.

Numeric metrics are TSV and interpretation is Markdown. Checkpoints and
smoke artifacts remain under ignored `outputs/` paths.
