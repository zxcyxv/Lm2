# Epoch-5 H1 shallow-quality result

The existing fixed 64-prompt producer was rerun with only the checkpoint
changed from step 10071 to step 16785.  Interpretation is restricted to H1 and
the first four generated bytes.

| H1 measure | Epoch 3 | Epoch 5 | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| validation NLL | 1.51911 | 1.48307 | -0.03604 |
| greedy first-byte accuracy | 59.375% | 62.500% | +3.125 pp |
| greedy aligned accuracy, first 4 bytes | 40.625% | 41.406% | +0.781 pp |
| greedy mean exact-prefix length | 1.547 | 1.766 | +0.219 byte |
| top-p first-byte accuracy | 56.250% | 59.375% | +3.125 pp |
| top-p aligned accuracy, first 4 bytes | 32.422% | 34.375% | +1.953 pp |

The improvement is real but small: two additional prompts have the correct
first greedy byte, and the 64 by 4 comparison gains two aligned greedy bytes.
NLL improves more consistently than top-1 accuracy; the separately recorded
validation H1 top-1 accuracy is 56.35% at epoch 3 and 55.37% at epoch 5, so a
broad categorical-quality jump is not supported.

Visible shallow improvements include:

- prefix ending `animal`: epoch 3 begins ` , and`, while epoch 5 produces the
  exact `s , and ` continuation for eight bytes;
- prefix ending `advoca`: epoch 3 begins `te`, while epoch 5 begins the exact
  `ted `;
- `against t -> he` and `Richar -> d` remain correct.

Persistent errors show the remaining limitation:

- the U2 context expects `punk`, but epoch 5 begins `the`;
- the Turkish-forces context begins `state of` instead of `Turkish`;
- `conti` in `separate contingents` is completed as `nued`, a valid suffix for
  another word but wrong for both syntax and context.

Therefore epoch 5 modestly sharpens immediate morphology, punctuation, and a
few short continuations.  It does not yet show a qualitative phase change in
semantic next-byte selection.  The H1 base is improving gradually rather than
accelerating into a new sentence-level regime by epoch 5.
