## Question

Can a proposal-conditioned spectral operator `T(q)` learn the model's own
greedy decode--reencode collapse as a behavioral distribution, when the
ordinary language CE continues to decode the uncertain raw proposal `q` and
never passes through T?

The predecessor regressed `T(q)q` directly onto the self-selected canonical
encoder state with relative MSE. It reduced Euclidean state error but decoded
the selected token from the corrected state at only `0.1210`. This experiment
replaces that MSE with a decoder-visible KL target and gives the student KL
gradient to K, T, the shared reversible encoder/inverse-decoder parameters,
and the tied token readout.

## Architecture

The backbone remains width 896 with two reversible causal encoder blocks, the
analytic exact-inverse decoder, and the RMS-tied embedding head.

K and T are distinct input-conditioned operators generated by distinct MLPs:

```text
K_A = MLP_K(h_A)
q_B = K_A h_A

T_B = MLP_T(q_B)
hhat_B = T_B q_B
```

`K_A` uses the existing prefix-conditioned normal spectral operator. Its MLP
reads `h_A` and emits paired decay and phase parameters in a learned global
orthogonal basis. `T_B` uses the existing identity-initialized width-128
spectral corrector. Its separate MLP reads `q_B` and emits bounded paired
log-amplitude and phase corrections in the same basis.

Only raw `q_B` is used for ordinary next-token CE:

```text
p_B = softmax(Head(Decode(prefix_A, q_B)))
```

The model's target-independent greedy action is
`b_B = argmax(p_B)`. The literal prefix and `b_B` are passed through the same
encoder to obtain the canonical self-selected state
`hstar_B = Encode(prefix_A, b_B)`. That state is immediately passed through
the exact inverse decoder and tied head to form the self-redecode teacher
distribution `r_B`.

The student distribution is obtained by decoding the corrected state:

```text
s_B = softmax(Head(Decode(prefix_A, T_B q_B)))
```

## Objective and gradient routing

```text
L_CE   = CE(p_B, gold_(A+1))
L_meta = KL(stopgrad(r_B) || s_B)
L      = L_CE + L_meta
```

- T is absent from `L_CE`; CE gradients to every T parameter are zero.
- The greedy action, canonical re-encoding, and teacher re-decoding graph are
  stop-gradient.
- The student input `q_B` is not detached. `L_meta` updates T, q/K, the
  shared spectral basis, the causal encoder prefix representation, the exact
  inverse decoder, and the tied readout.
- The teacher is used at its native temperature. Its near-one-hot confidence
  is intentional because it represents the model's already selected action,
  not the uncertain corpus conditional distribution.
- There is no latent MSE, horizon CE, gold-token canonical target, EMA
  teacher, branch loss, trajectory responsibility, or shell projection.

## Intended inference

Inference alternates the two learned operators sequentially without token
feedback inside the generated block:

```text
q_B = K(h_A) h_A
h_B = T(q_B) q_B
q_C = K(h_B) h_B
h_C = T(q_C) q_C
...
```

The corrected state tape `[h_B, h_C, ...]` is jointly passed through the
causal exact-inverse decoder. A matched block-4 audit compares this path with
raw greedy AR, which decodes, selects, and canonically re-encodes at every
token.

## Structural criteria

- K's controller reads `h_A`; T's distinct controller reads `q_B`.
- T is identity at initialization below relative error `1e-6`.
- changing the corpus future token does not change q, selected action,
  canonical teacher, corrected state, or either self-redecode distribution.
- the self-selected canonical state matches literal prefix-plus-action
  encoding below `5e-5`.
- the teacher distribution and canonical state require no gradient.
- CE gives zero or absent gradient to every T parameter.
- meta KL gives finite nonzero gradients to T, q/K, encoder/inverse-decoder,
  and tied embedding/head parameters.
- raw CE logits are decoded from q rather than `T(q)q`.
- all states, logits, losses, and gradients are finite.

## Step-1000 success criteria

Primary:

- validation one-token raw-q NLL is below its step-1 value;
- corrected teacher-to-student KL is lower than paired raw-q teacher KL;
- corrected/raw teacher-KL ratio is at most `0.50`;
- corrected-state top-1 agrees with the self-redecode teacher on at least
  `0.90` of validation positions.

Sequential inference:

- raw AR and alternating K--T agree on at least `0.90` of block-4 tokens;
- raw AR and alternating K--T agree on at least `0.75` of complete blocks.

Supporting diagnostics include teacher selected-token confidence, canonical
state relative MSE, gold NLL/accuracy from raw and corrected states, operator
scale/phase magnitudes, orthogonality error, throughput, and peak VRAM.

Numeric metrics are TSV and interpretation is Markdown. Checkpoints and
